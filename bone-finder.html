How to use
  1. Frame yourself. Stand or sit so your head, shoulders and hips are in view. Step back further to include your knees and feet.
  2. Make a pointing hand. Index finger straight, other fingers curled.
  3. Touch and hold. Rest the fingertip on your body. The ring around your fingertip fills, then the bone lights up and its details appear here.
  4. Hand bones. Hold your other hand up, palm to the camera, and point at its fingers, knuckles or wrist.
  5. Quiz mode asks you to find a bone. Left and right always mean your own left and right.
  6. Collect data records labeled examples for training. Download the CSV, train with train.py, then load model.json under Trained model.
